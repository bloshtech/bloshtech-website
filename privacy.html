1. Local Storage Only (Offline Architecture)

CutMaster Pro operates strictly on an offline-first model. All customer records, phone numbers, measurements, style notes, financial balances, and fabric photo swatches are saved locally on your physical device storage.

We do not collect, monitor, track, view, transmit, upload, or sell your customer records. BloshTech Educational Hub Ltd maintains no remote servers or external databases connected to your application ledger.

When you export a backup, an encrypted .json file is generated locally. You maintain full control over where that backup file is stored (e.g., your personal WhatsApp, Google Drive, or local device storage).
